# Store page (draft, for the Creator Hub)

Goal: stand apart from DONT SPLASH (see sprint 34 in [[Decisions]]). Lead with the rising water, not "don't splash".

## Name
**Hush Halls** (keep). Optional suffix while the trend is hot: "Hush Halls 🌊 Blind Monster".

## Description
> Every sound floods the halls.
> A blind monster hunts by sound, and every noise you make raises the water. Sprint and it hears you. Walk and the water rises slowly... but it still rises.
> Watch your feet: puddles splash, loose planks creak. Reach the exit before you're caught or drowned.
> - New hall layout every round
> - Play solo or with friends
> - Works on phone, tablet and PC

## Genre
Horror (the brief says Runner; Horror is where this audience searches).

## Icon (512×512) idea
The monster's eyeless face half-submerged, water line across the middle of the icon, one bright ripple. Dark teal and black, no text.

## Thumbnails
1. A player tiptoeing in a dark hall, the monster right behind, water at knee height. Caption: "EVERY SOUND FLOODS THE HALLS".
2. A splash in a puddle with sound rings and the monster turning toward it. Caption: "IT HEARS YOU".
3. Water almost to the ceiling, the exit door glowing. Caption: "REACH THE EXIT".
